Editor page .binding-row, .device-card, .picker, .expr-list

The binding editor: device cards, the two-line binding rows, kind tags, the picker and the expression autocomplete.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: hover, selected, problem; the full catalogue is on the states page.

A selected two-line binding row: control, event, kind tag, expression and the guard.

Binding row .binding-row

  • 11/14 padding, radius 8, a divider between rows
  • Hover --inset
  • .selected fills --cyan-bg with a 2px inset --cyan left bar
  • Line 1: the control (.ctrl) mono 13 600 (.problem in --amber), the event (.event) 12 --text-3, then a live value (.live, mono 12 --text-2) or a .warn triangle, pinned right
  • Line 2: kind pills, the expression (.expr, ellipsized; .none italic --text-3), and the guard (.when, 11.5 --text-3; .problem amber) pinned right

Kind tag .kind-chip

  • The page's own square tag: 11px 600, letter-spacing 0.05em, --chip-* colours, radius 4, 1.5/7 padding
  • Distinct from the rounded pill
  • It uses --chip-text rather than --text-3 because dusk's --text-3 measures 4.14:1 on --chip-bg, under the 4.5:1 standard

Device card .device-card

  • Matches the profiles card shape
  • A .section-label (11px 700 uppercase, 18/2/9 margins) above the "At load" stat card

Bindings head .bindings-head

  • A 20px 700 device name, a kind pill, a connection pill
  • The .count at the right edge

Picker .picker

  • Follows the devices page shape
  • The #actionPicker tightens its rows (2/0 margins, 8/0 padding) and adds a .selected state (cyan fill + left bar)

Action card .act-card

  • A committed action card that re-opens the editor on click (hover --hover-line)
  • The .card-del and .more buttons in its head are the per-action remove and options buttons (amber and --chip-bg hovers, 44px hits)

Source item .src-item

  • Rows divide with --line-soft
  • Reserve 36 of right padding for the hover-revealed .src-del (20px, amber on hover)

Expression list .expr-list

  • The autocomplete dropdown under the expression input
