Stat cards .stat-row, .stat

A row of summary numbers: each card holds a big mono number over a small label.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: problem; the full catalogue is on the states page.

Three stat cards: a plain number, an amber one, and a cyan one.

Row .stat-row

  • A grid of cards, 3 across by default, 10 apart, 12/16/4 padding
  • Two across at 900 and 640; four across on the monitor page
  • .two forces two across (devices)

Card .stat

  • --inset background, 1px --line-soft, radius 8, 10/12 padding

Number .n

  • 21px 700 --font-mono
  • .stat.amber and .stat.cyan take the role colour for the number

Label .l

  • 11px --text-3, 3 below the number
