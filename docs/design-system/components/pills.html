Pills .chip, .status-pill

Short labels in a fully rounded outline: the common pill shape. The base pill is the quiet tag; the status pill is the bolder version used in top bars and card heads.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: active, problem; the full catalogue is on the states page.

Variants

plain connected 1 left out active not connected written by bnd-fn X-Plane ready 1 left out The base pill variants (plain, ok, warn, active, off) and the two status pills.

Base pill .chip

  • 11px 500, radius 999, 2/9 padding, contents 6 apart, in the neutral --chip-bg / --chip-line / --chip-text colours
  • Lead-in shapes: a 6px filled .dot or a 7px hollow .ring

Colour variants

  • .ok (green family + dot)
  • .warn (amber family + a triangle mark)
  • .active (cyan family + dot)
  • .off (neutral + ring)

Page variants

  • .write (violet, mono 10.5) and .read (cyan, mono 10.5) on the variables page
  • .cyan / .vio event-kind pills on the monitor page

Status pill .status-pill

  • Contents 7 apart, radius 999, 5/12 padding, 12px 600, single line
  • The .dot is 7px in currentColor; at 640 wide it is 4/9 padding at 11px
  • The profiles page adds .status-pill.off (--text-3 on --inset, --line border) for "no profile running"

  .status-pill     Status pill
