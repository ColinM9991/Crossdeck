Devices page .device-card, .picker, .modal

The device list card, the add-device picker, the reassign modal, and the hardware-screen preview.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: hover, selected, problem, hidden; the full catalogue is on the states page.

The add-device picker: a card listing detected hardware.

Device card .device-card

  • Same shape as the profile card (selected = --cyan-line border + --selected-bg)
  • A wrapping .foot, a full-width .count, and the hover-revealed .row-del
  • The remove is 22px, hidden until the card is hovered or at 640, amber on hover, a 44px hit area

Picker .picker

  • Reuses the card: the .picker-list is 4/0 padding
  • Each .picker-row is 9/16 padding, radius 7, 6 in from the sides, hover --inset
  • The id (.pid) is mono 12.5 600 and the description (.pdesc) 12 --text-3
  • The .pplus add mark is a 14px 700 --cyan plus that fades in on hover (always visible at 640)
  • A .picker-empty note is 14/16 --text-3

Modal .modal

  • A full-screen --backdrop overlay, centred, above the page
  • The .modal-card is 440 wide (100 minus 32 at the edges), --card / --line, radius 12, --shadow-1, 16 padding, announced as a modal dialog
  • Its head is a 14px 700 title with a 26px .close (hover --text on --chip-bg)
  • .modal-help is 12px --text-3
  • .modal-list stacks .picker-rows 4 apart
  • Closes on a scrim click or Escape

Row and value variants

  • .stat-row.two forces the two-across grid
  • .kv .v.chips wraps pill values (mono 10.5)
  • A .hw-row (10 apart) shows .hw-id (12.5 --text-2, mono) and .hw-product (12 --text-2) beside a kind pill
  • .refs-empty is the italic "no hardware" line
  • The .detail-body is a single column (24 apart, top-aligned)
  • .with-preview adds a 320 wide preview column that collapses at 640

The FMC and segment screen previews. Their colours are fixed (not theme-driven) because they simulate the hardware's own display.

Fixed colours

  • The preview colours are fixed, not token-driven
  • They simulate the hardware's own display (FMC green #57e08a on #030504; segment amber on #141008)
  • They deliberately do not follow the theme

Layout

  • The .preview-body centres the screen (14 padding, 9 gap)
  • .preview-note is an 11px --text-3 caption

FMC screen .screen.fmc

  • 24:14 aspect, 9.5px tracked labels
  • A dashed #37453d divider
  • An 11px key row
  • Green #57e08a values on #030504

Segment screen .screen.seg

  • 10px 0.2em tracked keys at 55% amber
  • 26px 700 --amber values on #141008

Detail actions

  • .remove-action (this page's 12-above variant) and .open-action are the detail pane's full-width CTAs
  • .save-hint is the centred text variant
