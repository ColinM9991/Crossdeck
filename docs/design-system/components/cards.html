Cards .card, .card-h, .card-b, .card-foot

The content container: a header bar, a body region, and optional footer actions.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: hover; the full catalogue is on the states page.

Devices
A card with a head, a body of key-value rows, and a foot of link actions.

Card .card

  • --card background, 1px --line, radius 10, 14 below, --shadow-1
  • Contents clipped to the corners

Head .card-h

  • Contents 9 apart, 13/16 padding, 14px 700
  • A 1px --line-soft under-edge
  • A .sub line is 12px 400 --text-3
  • A .close action sits at the right edge: 15px --text-3, 0/6 padding, radius 6; hover --text on --chip-bg; a 44px hit area (editor page)

Body .card-b

  • 2/0 padding, a plain region
  • Rows such as key-value rows and setting rows supply their own padding; form bodies use 12/16

Foot .card-foot

  • Contents 14 apart, 9/16 padding, a 1px --line-soft top edge
  • A .link is 12.5px 600; a .ghost link is --text-3 hovering to --text-2
  • The profiles page right-aligns the foot
