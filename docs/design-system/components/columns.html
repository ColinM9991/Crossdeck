Columns and heads .col, .col-head

The page's content grid. Each page sets its own column widths (e.g. a 92 / 1fr / 420 three-pane layout); the columns are the scrollable panes and the heads are their title rows.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

Anatomy

Column .col

  • The scrollable pane: 20/20/28 padding (top / left-right / bottom)
  • Detail panes add a --panel background; adjacent panes divide with a --line-soft border
  • At 1180 wide the columns stack into rows and scrolling becomes visible; at 640 there is a single column

Head .col-head

  • The pane's title row, 10 apart, 14 below
  • Title 18px 700 -0.01em
  • A .sub line 12px --text-3
  • A .actions group (page rule) at the right edge, 8 apart

Structure

  .col             Column
  .col-head        Head
