Rail .rail, .rail-item

Cross-page navigation: the 92px icon column on the left, which becomes a fixed bottom nav at 640 wide.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: hover, active, hidden; the full catalogue is on the states page.

Anatomy

The icon rail: one item per page, the active item in the cyan treatment with a count badge.

Rail .rail

  • The 92px column
  • A 1px --line-soft edge, items centred and 6 apart, 16 top / 14 bottom padding
  • Announced as the primary navigation

Item .rail-item

  • 72 wide, 9 vertical padding, radius 10, 11px in --text-3, with 19px stroke-1.7 icons
  • The Variables item uses a mono 14px 600 "{ }" glyph (.glyph-braces)
  • Hover: --text-2 on --inset
  • The active item is --cyan on --cyan-bg with a 3px rounded --cyan indicator 14 out from the left edge (7 inset top/bottom)
  • At 640 wide the indicator moves to the item's bottom edge, 24 wide

Badge .rail-badge

  • A count tag at the item's top-right (4 / 10 in)
  • --chip-bg / --chip-line / --chip-text, 9px 700, radius 999, 0/5 padding, 13 line height

Push .push

  • Pins the last item (Settings) to the rail's foot
  • Hidden in the bottom nav

640 bottom nav

  • Fixed to the left, right and bottom edges
  • --panel background, --line-soft top edge, safe-area padding
  • Items 52 wide / 48 tall minimum, 2 apart
  • The body reserves 66 of bottom padding
