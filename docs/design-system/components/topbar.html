Top bar .topbar

The 56px band across every page: wordmark, active profile, connection state, unsaved changes, and the primary actions.

Notation: a/b figures are vertical/horizontal (padding, or the gap when followed by apart); radius N is a border radius in px; 13px 600 is font size and weight; 11px in --text-3 names the colour token; a bare --token is defined on the Tokens page.

States this component has: hover, problem, pulsing, hidden; the full catalogue is on the states page.

The live top bar: brand, the active profile pill, connection status, the unsaved count and the primary action.

Band

  • 56px tall, --chrome background, --chrome-shadow, a 1px --line-soft bottom edge
  • Contents 14 apart with 16 left/right padding
  • The right-hand controls sit at the far edge

Brand .brand

  • The wordmark
  • 17px, 700, letter-spacing -0.02em, 92 wide, fixed

Profile pill .profile-chip

  • A card-surface pill: --card fill, 1px --line, radius 8, 7/12 padding, 300 minimum wide, contents 10 apart
  • Name 13px 600; sim line 12px --text-3; a chevron at the right edge in --text-3
  • Hover: border --hover-line
  • At 640 wide it fills the row and the sim line hides

Status and unsaved

  • A status pill (see Pills) beside the unsaved counter
  • The counter is --amber, 13px 600, announced as a status region, and hidden at zero
  • It pulses (a 0.45s scale) on each change
